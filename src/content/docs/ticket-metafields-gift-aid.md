---
title: "Ticket Metafields: Gift Aid"
---
You can also set **Gift Aid eligibility** using the Variant Metafield block. Once you have created your ticket variants, select the relevant ticket, navigate to the **Foyer - Giftaidable** field, and enable Gift Aid for **eligible** ticket variants by selecting **true**. Select **false** for ticket variants that are **not eligible** for Gift Aid.  
  
This displays an automatically generated **Gift Aid declaration text** to customers when they select an eligible ticket variant.
