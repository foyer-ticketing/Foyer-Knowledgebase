- After a customer has added their tickets to their cart, you can **suggest additional items** to **add** before checkout. This could be related museum/exhibition merchandise such as tote bags or catalogues.
- Use the **Foyer - Upsell Heading** and **Foyer - Upsell Text fields** to customise the text shown with your suggested item.
- Upload your product image to the **Foyer - Upsell Product** field. This is required for your upsell suggestion to display.
