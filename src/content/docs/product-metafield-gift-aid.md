---
title: "Product Metafield: Gift Aid"
---
If all of your ticket variants are eligible for Gift Aid then fill in the **Foyer - Gift Aid Heading** and **Foyer - Gift Aid Text** fields to customise the Gift Aid message shown to customers.

:::note
Gift Aid eligible tickets need to be enabled in the ticket variants metafield when [setting up your tickets](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lGic6SQkFC6y8nnMlYd3s9T_42hpD0P1WMlSSH3Z9U8/edit?tab=t.a5kdcq1m9t46#bookmark=id.3n3385uuzhve).
:::
