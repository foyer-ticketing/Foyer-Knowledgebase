---
title: "Product Metafield: Gift Aid"
---
If all of your ticket variants are eligible for Gift Aid then fill in the **Foyer - Gift Aid Heading** and **Foyer - Gift Aid Text** fields to customise the Gift Aid message shown to customers.

:::note
Gift Aid eligible tickets need to be enabled in the ticket variants metafield when setting up your tickets.
:::
