Once you have created your ticket variants, you can add further detail to each variant in the Metafields block. You can see a list of Foyer metafields [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lGic6SQkFC6y8nnMlYd3s9T_42hpD0P1WMlSSH3Z9U8/edit?tab=t.a5kdcq1m9t46#bookmark=id.q7yxcms19l2r).


:::note
The instructions here cover the metafields relevant to setting up your tickets. The remaining metafields are reserved for app administration and are configured internally for higher-tier plans.
:::
